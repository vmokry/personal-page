Events FAQ

I host events at my place fairly often, and the same questions keep coming up – so I wrote the answers down in one place.

  • Is the event happening? Yes! My events are always happening, so I don’t reply to this one on the day – I’m busy entertaining people, preparing food, or cleaning glasses. I’ve cancelled only one party ever, and that one was paid.
  • Should I RSVP? Not necessary, but welcome.
  • How many people do you expect? No idea. Sometimes there are 6 of us, sometimes over 20. What matters is the quality of conversation, not the quantity.
  • Can I come with a cool friend or partner? Yes. For your partner, there are no rules, but for friends, please bring someone who is cooler than you.
  • Can I bring my dog? No. I mean, you don’t have a Shiba Inu, do you?
  • Can I come later? Why not, but after 11pm it’s borderline. Some events end early, especially if they fall in the middle of the week – people need to catch the metro and get some good sleep for the next day.
  • Can I bring anything? BYOB, and anything you would like to share. No obligation – there will be tea, some drinks, and snacks / canapés.
  • Do I need to take my shoes off? Yes.
  • Can I smoke here? Technically, no. But if you’re very discreet, you can step onto my back balcony – just do it quickly and quietly.
  • Can I unsubscribe from your invites? Sure. Send me a human-made, humorous “unsubscribe” message and I’ll remove you from the guest list.

And that’s all, folks! See you soon.
