Events FAQ

I host events at my place fairly often, and I keep answering the same questions. So, here they are – once and for all.

  • Is the event happening? On purpose, I do not reply to this question throughout the evening – I am busy entertaining people, preparing food, or cleaning glasses. My events are always happening. I have cancelled only one party ever – and that one was paid, so I had really high expectations.
  • How many people do you expect? I have no idea. Sometimes there are 6 of us, sometimes over 20. What matters is the quality of conversation, not the quantity.
  • Can I come later? Why not, but after 11pm it is borderline. Some events end early, especially if they fall in the middle of the week – people need to catch the metro and get some good sleep for the day after.
  • Can I bring anything? BYOD, and anything you would like to share. No obligation – there will be tea, some drinks, and snacks / canapés.
  • Can I come with a cool friend or partner? Yes. For your partner, there are no rules, but for friends, please bring someone that is cooler than you.
  • Can I smoke here? Technically, no. But if you are very discreet, you can quickly (and quietly) step onto my back balcony.
  • Should I RSVP? Not necessary, but welcome.
  • Do I need to take my shoes off? Yes.
  • Can I bring my dog? No.
  • Can I unsubscribe from your invites? Sure. Send me a human-made, humorous "unsubscribe" message and I will remove you from the guest list.

And that is all, folks!
