About

“Sometimes I draw what I want to do. The rest of the time, I draw what I have to.”

Summary

  • Senior Concept Artist with a passion for creating immersive worlds. (previously Respawn/EA, Unity, Treehouse Digital, Volta)
  • Art Director and Game Designer delivering ideas for indie games. (previously Studio Imugi, Thamira: The Sorceress, Purgatory)
  • Instructor at concept art schools and mentor to junior artists.
  • Former Entrepreneur and UX Lead with years of leadership experience and a track record of building successful B2B and B2C products.
  • Credits: Dragon Age: The Veilguard, Apex Legends, Call of Duty: Vanguard, Gigaya, Sunday Gold + many undisclosed AAA projects.
